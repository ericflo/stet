Leaderboard

The standings of the competition. Every entry is a data editor: a program that edits each round's raw corpus before a fixed model trains on it. Entries are ranked by mean gain over the unedited corpus: how much lower the model's exam loss is after the editor has been at the data. Fresh corpus every round, so nothing here was tuned to one sample.
