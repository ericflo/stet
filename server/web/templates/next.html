What is in it

About 1,500 items a round, each from a different corner of a very large map: a topic, in a format, for a kind of task, in a language. Arithmetic and word problems, programs and what they print, an agent's shell sessions and tool calls, lookups in tables and JSON, logic, grammar, format conversions, new Wikipedia articles and additions in every language, new questions and answers. Languages come in the shares the round's own pages have.

Every item is scored by how likely the model finds the right text, never by picking a choice: at this size the model's choices are near chance, but the likelihood of the right answer moves a long way. A curator's score is its gain over the unedited pages on the same items, from the same starting weights.
