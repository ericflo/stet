Volunteers do it for nothing but the standings on this page; the organisers' own machines are marked, and count as one person.

How they keep each other honest

Grading runs nobody's code but the harness

What a grader downloads is an edit script, which is data: a list of pieces of pages to copy. The harness checks it line by line. Running entrants' programs is a separate job you ask for, given only to established verifiers and run in a sandbox with no network.

Standing is earned

Every grade is sealed until its round settles. A script's gain is the median of the established verifiers' grades of it, and everyone else's grade is compared with that; the round's page shows every one, whatever it said. After {{$d.EstablishAfter}} grades that agreed, an account is established. An account whose grades differ {{$d.MuteAfter}} times, and more than once for every ten that agreed, is given no work for {{$d.MuteDays}} days.

Nobody is taken at their word

Each entry is run by {{$d.RunsPerEntry}} different people, and a third when they differ. Nobody runs their own entry, and no volunteer grades a script that only they wrote. When the organisers' machine is the only one there, it grades its own runs, and those scores say they are not confirmed.

Verifiers go first

When more curators are waiting than a round can take, the entries of people who verified in the last two weeks are drawn first.
