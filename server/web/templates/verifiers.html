How they keep each other honest

Grading runs nobody's code

What a grader downloads is an edit script, which is data: a list of pieces of pages to copy. Running entrants' programs is a separate job, given only to established verifiers and run in a sandbox with no network.

Standing is earned

Every grade is sealed until its round settles, then compared with the established verifiers' median. After {{$d.EstablishAfter}} grades that agreed, an account is established. One whose grades differ {{$d.MuteAfter}} times, and more than once for every ten that agreed, gets no work for {{$d.MuteDays}} days.

Nobody is taken at their word

Each entry is run by {{$d.RunsPerEntry}} different people, and a third when they differ. Nobody runs their own entry. When the organisers' machine is the only one there, it grades its own runs, and those scores say they are not confirmed.

Verifiers go first

When more curators are waiting than a round can take, the entries of people who verified in the last two weeks are drawn first.
